## Use a configurable product with variants

1. Create or open a *Quotation*.
2. Click on *Add a configurable product*.
3. Tick *With variants?* and select the product template (the product and its
   variants must exist, e.g. the T-shirt in sizes M and L).
4. In the *Variants* section, add one line per variant and set the quantity.
   The parent line quantity is updated automatically: it is the sum of the
   quantities of all the variants.
5. Set the price on each variant line.

The discounts based on the quantity are applied on the total quantity of the
parent line (the sum of the quantities of all the variants). The variants are
the delivered products.
